### The three pillars of observability in your own words

- Metrics : Describes what went wrong in the system such is cpu is over loaded or server is down etc.

- Logs : Demonstrates details of issues when it went wrong.

- Traces : list down the step of the process and identify where something went wrong.

### Explanation of counter vs gauge with examples

counter: It counts the number request and goes up only because it keep adding the requests.
gauge: It gauges the performance of system such as cpu usage, request latency, memory space whats why it keep going up and down.
